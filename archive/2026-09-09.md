`score 85` · Research & papers · 🇬🇧 English · via Google DeepMind 官方博客

Google DeepMind released and made public the AlphaGenome Atlas on September 8, 2026, a searchable database that pre-calculates the molecular effects of approximately 9 billion single-base mutations in the human genome, with a scale of approximately 1 PB (30 times larger than the AlphaFold database).
